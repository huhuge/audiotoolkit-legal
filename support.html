Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert an audio file?

Open AudioToolkit, choose the conversion feature, select a supported file, choose an available output format, and start the conversion. Available formats may vary by app version.

How do I trim or compress audio?

Choose Trim or Compress, select an audio file, adjust the available options, and start processing. Keep the original file until you have confirmed that the exported file works as expected.

How do I extract audio from a video?

Choose the video-to-audio feature, select a video that AudioToolkit supports, choose an available output option, and export the audio.

Why did a file fail to import or process?

Check that the file is accessible, is not damaged, and uses a supported format. Make sure your device has enough free storage, then try again. If the problem continues, contact support with the error message and steps to reproduce it.

Where are my exported files?

Check the export destination shown by the app. Depending on the workflow, you may be able to save the result to Files or share it with another app.

How do subscriptions and purchases work?

Subscription prices, renewal periods, and any available trial are shown on the purchase screen before you confirm. You can manage or cancel an Apple-billed subscription in your Apple Account subscription settings. Use Restore Purchases in the app to restore eligible purchases.

Does support need my audio files?

Usually not. Please start by sharing the app version, iOS version, a description of the problem, and any error message. Do not include recordings or transcripts unless support specifically needs them and you choose to provide them.
